language, vision, speech, video, and agentic AI systems.

## Why this course exists

Many students can import a model but cannot explain why it fails, how to
evaluate it, or how to ship it responsibly. Many courses stop at a notebook
demo. This course closes that gap with:

- A build-first sequence that starts with training small models from scratch.
- Explicit data, licensing, privacy, and evaluation work in every applied
  module.
- Hardware-honest labs that run on Windows 11 and free GPU tiers.
- Published rubrics so that students can assess their own evidence.
- A capstone that resembles real engineering work, including documentation,
  security review, and a demo.

## Teaching approach

The course follows a consistent loop: short theory, a guided notebook, an
independent lab, and a reflection. The instructor's role is to define the
problem, the constraints, and the standard of evidence; the student's role is to
build, measure, and explain.

The materials favor free and open-source tools, reproducible environments, and
honest limitations over polished but unverifiable claims.
