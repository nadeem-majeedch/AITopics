# AITopics licensing

AITopics is **multi-licensed**. No single licence covers every file. The
machine-readable scope is [`licensing-scope.json`](../licensing-scope.json);
this page explains it for students and downstream users. It is a provenance
summary, not legal advice.

Copyright in original AITopics material is held by
**Muhammad Nadeem Majeed**, whose legal name was confirmed by the instructor.
Honorifics are omitted from the licence line. The MIT notice reads
`Copyright (c) 2026 Muhammad Nadeem Majeed`.

**Commercial reuse is allowed** for original educational content under CC BY
4.0, provided the attribution and notice requirements are met. MIT also permits
commercial use of original code.

## What each licence covers

### MIT: original software

`LICENSE` applies to original source code written for AITopics, including Python
programs, tests, utility scripts, and repository build/configuration files. It
does **not** apply to third-party dependencies, vendored code, datasets, media,
or pretrained model weights. Keep the copyright and permission notice when you
copy or substantially reuse the code.

### CC BY 4.0: original educational content

`LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt` applies to original lessons, documentation, labs,
Jupyter notebooks (including their explanatory text and original outputs),
templates, diagrams, and other teaching material. You may share, adapt, and use
this material commercially, including in paid courses, provided you:

1. credit **Muhammad Nadeem Majeed** and AITopics;
2. link to the CC BY 4.0 licence;
3. indicate whether you changed the material; and
4. do not imply that the author endorses your use.

A suggested attribution:

> Adapted from AITopics by Muhammad Nadeem Majeed, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
> Changes: [describe changes]. Source: https://github.com/nadeem-majeedch/AITopics

### CC0 1.0: original synthetic datasets

`LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt` applies only to original synthetic datasets generated by
repository code from fixed seeds, after the provenance review recorded in
[`SYNTHETIC-DATA.md`](SYNTHETIC-DATA.md). The P06 object images are the verified
example: they are rendered from NumPy primitives, contain no third-party images,
and are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required
by CC0, but citing the course and preserving the synthetic provenance is good
practice.

CC0 does **not** apply to third-party datasets, downloaded images or audio,
pretrained weights, or model checkpoints derived from third-party weights.

## Third-party material is excluded

This repository does not relicense third-party material. In particular:

- **Dependencies** such as NumPy, pandas, PyTorch, torchvision, OpenCV, and
  MkDocs are installed under their own licences and are not vendored here.
- **External datasets** such as COCO, CIFAR-10, and DeepFashion remain under
  their upstream terms. They are not distributed in this repository.
- **Pretrained weights** such as SmolLM2, Qwen, and torchvision SSDLite COCO
  weights are downloaded at runtime and remain subject to their own terms.
  AITopics does not redistribute those weight files.
- **SSDLite specifically:** the torchvision implementation code is
  BSD-3-Clause. The COCO pretrained weights have **no separate weight licence
  stated by torchvision**, and torchvision warns that weights may inherit
  dataset-derived terms. The weights are therefore **not** MIT, BSD-3-Clause, or
  CC BY 4.0 by virtue of this repository's licences; their status is unresolved.
- **External images, audio, video, and quoted excerpts** remain under their
  original terms and must carry their own attribution.

See the [licensing and attribution register](../docs/technical/licensing-and-attribution.md)
for the evidence, source links, and unresolved items.

## How to identify the licence for a file

1. Read an explicit `SPDX-License-Identifier` or copyright notice in the file or
   its directory. A specific notice overrides the general map.
2. Otherwise consult [`licensing-scope.json`](../licensing-scope.json), which
   lists the approved path scopes, exclusions, and unresolved items.
3. If the file is third-party material, use the upstream licence even if it sits
   inside an AITopics directory.
4. If a file is not covered by an explicit scope and is not clearly third-party,
   open an issue rather than assuming a licence. Do not infer a licence from a
   file extension alone.
